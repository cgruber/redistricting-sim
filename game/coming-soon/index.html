Past the Post

An educational redistricting simulator.

Draw district boundaries, simulate elections, and discover how the same voters can produce wildly different outcomes depending on where the lines are drawn.

Not advocacy for or against any party, position, or reform. It presents multiple perspectives — partisan operatives, reform commissioners, bipartisan dealmakers — so players leave with better questions, not predetermined answers.

Want a sneak-peek? Check out beta.pastthepost.gg.

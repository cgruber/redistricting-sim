## Summary

Add a cosmetic **city-limits** overlay to the map, exposed as a third entry in the
right-side View toolbar's Overlays group (alongside county borders). It draws a
boundary around the dense urban core(s) of a scenario — "where the city is" — with no
effect on gameplay, contiguity, or scoring. This is the prerequisite for **tutorial-003**
("Reading the Vote", GAME-098), which teaches the lean / county / **city** view set.

## Current State

- The View toolbar (`#map-filters`, right side) has a Color radiogroup
  (districts / lean) and an Overlays group with a single toggle: county borders
  (`filter-county`, cosmetic dashed border driven by `county_id`).
- County borders are generated by the pipeline county stage (flood-fill →
  `county_id` per precinct) and rendered as dashed borders between differing ids.
- There is **no** city concept in the model or the renderer.

## Approach

- Reuse the county border mechanism. A city is a cosmetic grouping of precincts; the
  overlay draws a border around the urban precinct set, the same way county borders
  are drawn around differing `county_id` values.
- Source of the city set: derive from the population field (a density threshold over
  the urban core, reusing the county stage's `carveCore`-style range-relative contour),
  exposed as `city_id` (or a boolean `in_city`) per precinct. Cosmetic only — like
  `county_id`, it must not feed the simulation, contiguity, or any criterion.
- Toolbar: add a second Overlays toggle (`filter-city`), `aria-pressed`, localStorage
  persistence, theme tooltip, mirroring the county toggle.

## Goals / Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Model/pipeline carries a cosmetic city designation per precinct (e.g. `city_id`),
      generated from the population field; never read by sim/contiguity/criteria.
- [ ] Renderer draws a city-limits border overlay (distinct from county dashed border).
- [ ] View toolbar gains a `filter-city` Overlays toggle (aria-pressed, persisted, tooltip).
- [ ] No gameplay effect: scoring/contiguity/validity identical with overlay on or off.
- [ ] Unit: city designation derives deterministically from a fixture population field.
- [ ] e2e: toggling city overlay shows/hides the border; state persists across reload.
